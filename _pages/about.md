I am interested in the intersection of quantum information and quantum foundations.

I completed my Ph.D. in Physics (Quantum Information) at the University of Waterloo, where I was affiliated with the [Institute for Quantum Computing](https://uwaterloo.ca/institute-for-quantum-computing/). I defended my [thesis](https://uwspace.uwaterloo.ca/items/b2ae2854-5dd1-4df4-b1e7-ef848fe1761b) on March 26, 2026. I was co-supervised by [Prof. Shohini Ghose](https://www.wlu.ca/academics/faculties/faculty-of-science/faculty-profiles/shohini-ghose/index.html) and [Prof. Robert Mann](https://uwaterloo.ca/physics-astronomy/profile/rbmann).
